About Us

Crafted in Granada

A boutique production house built on light, landscape, and thirty years of print expertise.

We translate complex creative concepts into iconic visual assets.

Granada Production House is a boutique photoshoot production company based in Granada, Spain. We specialise in fashion, commercial, editorial and advertising print shoots, alongside film production and events.

Our work is shaped by the place we call home — historic streets, coastal light, alpine horizons, and the quiet precision of print craft. From first treatment to final ink, we protect the creative vision while delivering production that holds up under real-world pressure.

The landscape

Granada is our backyard. We offer a curated portfolio of locations to provide the perfect light and architectural depth for your next campaign.

The craft

Specialised consultancy for print and production. With thirty years in the industry, we ensure the technical integrity of your project from the first shot to the final ink on paper.
